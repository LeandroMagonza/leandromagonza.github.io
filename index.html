About Me

I'm a Fullstack Developer with 8 years of professional experience. Along the way I spent 4 years working as a Unity Game Developer, and I've since returned to fullstack development — bringing that game-systems mindset back to the web.

I have experience designing board games, running live events such as treasure hunts and escape rooms, and programming video games: turn-based and real-time, single-player and online multiplayer (with Photon).

I mainly focus on game systems, data handling, architecture and algorithms. I hold a bachelor's degree in Computer Systems. I'm a native Spanish speaker with advanced proficiency in English.
